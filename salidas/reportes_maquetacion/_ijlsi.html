About the journal

The International Journal of Legal Science and Innovation is an open access, double-blind peer-reviewed journal of original scholarship at the meeting point of law, technology and emerging legal thought, published bi-monthly by VidhiAagaz since 2019.

It welcomes research articles, short notes, case comments and book reviews from academics, practitioners and students, and reports every manuscript’s progress live, from submission to publication.
